FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is it really free to start?

Yes — no credit card required. The Free plan gives you one league, live roster view, and player trend tracking. You can use it all season at no cost and upgrade whenever you want more tools.

Does this work with my Yahoo Fantasy Baseball league?

Yes. The Fantasy Lab is built specifically for Yahoo Fantasy Baseball. Connect your Yahoo account and your leagues and rosters are imported automatically. Both Head-to-Head and Rotisserie scoring formats are fully supported.

Do I need to install anything?

Nothing. The Fantasy Lab runs entirely in your browser. Sign up, connect your Yahoo account, and you're in — on any device.

How is this different from Yahoo's built-in tools?

Yahoo shows you raw numbers — we tell you what to do with them. The Fantasy Lab adds trending signals on every player, a 7-day pitcher forecast pulled from ESPN and cross-referenced with your league's available pool, a multi-filter waiver finder, hitter matchup tables, and a daily AI recommendation. None of that is built into Yahoo.

What does the Pickup Analyzer do?

It looks at your current roster and tells you which free agent you should add based on what your team actually needs. It's available on paid plans and takes the guesswork out of waiver decisions.

Is my league data safe?

Yes. We only read your Yahoo league data to power the tools — we never share it or sell it. Your account connects through Yahoo's official OAuth system, the same secure process Yahoo uses for all approved third-party apps.
